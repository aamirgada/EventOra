About This Experience

8th Annual Edition

Prepare for the Northern California's most spellbinding twilight gathering. Neon Horizon 2025 returns to the iconic Golden Gate Park Meadow, transmuting historic natural grounds into a luminous multi-dimensional playground of sound, light, and shared euphoria.

Featuring an unparalleled audio-visual installation programmed across three handcrafted stages, the festival unites international electronic pioneers, underground synthwave virtuosos, and boundary-pushing visual artists for an 8-hour unbroken journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

Full refunds are guaranteed automatically up to 7 calendar days before the event gate opens (until Friday, July 11, 2025 at 11:59 PM PDT). Tickets can also be securely transferred or resold via the official Eventora Fan-to-Fan exchange at zero markup fees.
This event is strictly 18 and over. You must present a valid government-issued photo ID (driver’s license, passport, or state ID card). Temporary paper documents and school IDs will not be accepted at entry scanners.
Prohibited items include outside beverages (except empty reusable hydration packs/flasks up to 32oz), weapons or sharp objects, professional DSLR cameras with detachable lenses, drone cameras, and illegal substances. Empty reusable bottles are welcome.
To guarantee swift and secure gate security, clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags smaller than 12" x 6" x 12" are approved. Small clutches or fanny packs no larger than 4.5" x 6.5" do not need to be clear.
